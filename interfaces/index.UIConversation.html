    Interface UIConversation

    The conversation and message shapes Facets['messages'] hands an app. MICA-172 — see ./accounts.ts.

    Both extend a @mica/shared/types row the SDK already imports, so moving them in acquired no new dependency: the wire shape stays shared with server/, and what is added here is the UI's own projection of it.

    Nobody else's identity is in either (MICA-339). A conversation carries no creator citizenid and no participant_a/participant_b; each of participants is the number on the phone in the thread (phone) and whether it is the reader's own (self); a message says whether the reader sent it (mine) and which member did (sender_id, a participants[].id) instead of carrying the sender's citizenid. A name is the reader's own contact for that number, or the number.

    Optionalabsent_number

    absent_number?: string | null

    The number on the other side of a 1:1 nobody else is in (MICA-350): the phone that left it, or was taken out of it by a privacy delete, or a number nobody held when the thread was started. No live member is there to carry it, so without this the reader sees no number at all. Absent on a group, a line thread, and a 1:1 both sides are still in.
